# Product Overview

**ImpactAlign SDG** is an AI-assisted platform that connects real-world activities and evidence to the
Sustainable Development Goals, with human validation and, eventually, accreditation.

## Who it is for

Entrepreneurs, professionals, professors, researchers, organizations, institutions, practitioners,
partners and assessors.

## The chain it makes verifiable

```
SDG Goal → SDG Target → SDG Indicator → Evidence → Impact → Validation → Accreditation
```

## Methodology

**Diagnose → Align → Measure → Validate → Improve**

## Principles

1. **Human-in-the-loop.** AI may suggest; it never makes the final validation or accreditation decision.
   AI output is always labelled as AI-generated.
2. **Organization data is isolated.** One organization can never see another's private data.
3. **Privacy by design.** Data minimisation, purpose limitation, consent where required, export and deletion.
4. **Honest claims.** No invented certifications, legal claims or official SDG data.
5. **Accessible and international.** Responsive, keyboard-accessible, semantic, ready for localisation.

## Brand and navigation

- Product name: **ImpactAlign SDG**; primary call to action: **JOIN IMPACTALIGN**
- Navigation order: HOME · ABOUT · SDGs · HOW IT WORKS · PLATFORM · RESOURCES · COMMUNITY · LOGIN · JOIN IMPACTALIGN
- Legal pages (placeholders): /privacy /terms /cookies /ai-ethics /data-protection /accessibility /security /acceptable-use /disclaimer

## What exists today (Phase 1)

Foundation, a redesigned public site (home + section and topic pages, all clearly marked placeholders), a dashboard
**interface preview** (15 routes, empty states, no data), a placeholder admin app, CI and local Docker data stores.
**No authentication, stored data, submissions or SDG content yet.**

## Earlier prototype

A separate working prototype (Express + SQLite, vanilla JS) explored registration, seven submission types and
a four-step wizard. It is not part of this repository; its submission types and wizard configuration are
candidates to port in the dashboard/evidence phase.
